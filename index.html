01 — THE STORY

SHE CAME UP IN OAKLAND,
WHERE THE ODDS WERE ALREADY STACKED.

Before she was grown, Ronique Marshall had survived homelessness, physical abuse, a parent's addiction, and a pregnancy she carried while still a child herself. What could have been the whole story became the first chapter instead.

Then the cameras came. On BET's Keyshia Cole: The Way It Is, America met the friend who told the truth when the truth wasn't convenient. This book is what happened before the cameras — and everything she built after they stopped rolling.
